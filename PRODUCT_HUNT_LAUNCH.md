### Étape 2 : Description courte (Short Description)
```text
ToonDraw is a joyful, zero-ad interactive drawing and animation studio for kids. Built entirely with vanilla web technologies, canvas, and Web Audio synthesis, it lets children draw with magic brushes, color superheroes & dinos, apply animated blinking stickers, and bring their art to life with playful sounds, speech cheer, and split-screen duo mode! Works offline on iPad & smartphones (PWA).
```

### Étape 3 : Premier Commentaire du Créateur (Maker's Comment)
*(À poster dès que votre page Product Hunt est publiée)*

```text
👋 Hi Product Hunt community!

I'm thrilled to share ToonDraw, an open-source, zero-ad creative studio built to make digital drawing fun, interactive, and completely safe for kids.

💡 Why did I build this?
As a parent and creator, I noticed most drawing apps for kids are cluttered with intrusive ads, paywalls, or heavy downloads. I wanted to build something clean, lightweight, accessible from any browser with zero installation, and dedicated to two wonderful boys: Ayham & Laith.

✨ What makes ToonDraw special?
🎨 Magic Canvas & Smart Fill: Rainbow pens, symmetry mirror, sparkle spray, and flood fill.
👀 Living Stickers & Animations: Blinking cartoon eyes, jumping stickers, and a "Give Life" mode where drawings float and dance.
🦸 Coloring Book: Preloaded superheroes, dinosaurs, and fantasy creatures.
🤝 Split-Screen Duo Mode: Allows two kids (or parent & child) to draw together simultaneously on the same screen.
🎵 100% Procedural Audio: All cartoon sounds (boing, pop, tada) are generated on the fly via Web Audio API—no heavy MP3 downloads.
🗣️ Warm Voice Cheer: Positive voice feedback and Arabic encouragements.
🔒 Safe & Offline Ready: 0 ads, 0 data tracking, 100% free, and installable as an offline PWA on iPad, Android, and desktop.

🚀 Live app: https://toondraw.vercel.app
⭐ Open source on GitHub: https://github.com/mormox2/ToonDraw

I’d love to hear your feedback, feature ideas, or thoughts on expanding creative tools for children! 🎨✨
```
